RENDER LANGUAGE (must match exactly, this is a fixed house style for this batch):
Flat 2D game asset concept, strictly orthogonal front view — no isometric, no three-quarter, no perspective, no camera tilt. Every material is rendered as FLAT POSTERIZED VALUE STEPS: distinct flat hard-edged color bands (4-5 per material — shadow / body / lit / rim), like a limited-palette indexed illustration. NOT a smooth gradient, NOT airbrushed, NOT painterly continuous blending — each band is one flat, uniform color with a hard boundary to the next band. No ink outline or contour line anywhere; bands separate from each other and from the background by color-value alone. No cast shadow, no floor plane, no backdrop scenery, no vignette, no dramatic studio lighting, no ambient occlusion gradient — the subject sits isolated on a single flat, uniformly toned dark neutral background (flat mid-dark blue-grey, no gradient in the background). Authored in its fully warm, lit-material appearance — never desaturated, never cooled, never tinted toward blue-grey (that is a separate derived state computed later, not something to render now).

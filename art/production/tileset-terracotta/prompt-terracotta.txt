CRITICAL: no outline or contour line of any kind around any tile. Tiles separate from each other purely by soft flat color-value difference at the joint — never a drawn dark ink line. Flat painterly shading, gouache-like, edges are soft value transitions only.

Palette — strictly these four warm reddish-orange terracotta values, used as flat color patches: shadow #5c2b1f, body #8c3f2a, lit face #b85a35, rim highlight #d98149 (sparingly, on a few worn tile edges/corners only). Surface detail: a few hairline stress cracks and small chipped corners as soft value marks, subtle warm mottling — never linework or hatching.

Forbidden: any outline/contour line; bright neon color; brass/gold/metal color; glow; text or watermark; photographic realism.

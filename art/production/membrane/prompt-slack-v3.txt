RENDER LANGUAGE (must match exactly, this is a fixed house style for this batch):
Flat 2D game asset concept, strictly orthogonal front view — no isometric, no three-quarter, no perspective, no camera tilt. Every material is rendered as FLAT POSTERIZED VALUE STEPS: distinct flat hard-edged color bands (4-5 per material — shadow / body / lit / rim), like a limited-palette indexed illustration. NOT a smooth gradient, NOT airbrushed, NOT painterly continuous blending — each band is one flat, uniform color with a hard boundary to the next band. No ink outline or contour line anywhere; bands separate from each other and from the background by color-value alone. No cast shadow, no floor plane, no backdrop scenery, no vignette, no dramatic studio lighting, no ambient occlusion gradient — the subject sits isolated on a single flat, uniformly toned dark neutral background (flat mid-dark blue-grey, no gradient in the background). Authored in its fully warm, lit-material appearance — never desaturated, never cooled, never tinted toward blue-grey (that is a separate derived state computed later, not something to render now).

Subject: a wide circular drum-skin disc set flush into a floor gap — a "membrane" — SLACK/default state. Flat orthogonal view straight down the barrel of the disc as if looking directly at a coin lying flat, filling most of the frame — no three-quarter or angled perspective, no ellipse foreshortening, a true flat circle.

NO staging: no cast shadow, no floor plane, no wall/backdrop scenery around it — the disc sits on the flat uniform background with nothing else in frame.

The hoop: a ring of dull oxidized bronze-green metal (#4d6152 flat shadow band, #6f8f74 flat verdigris body band — flat posterized bands, hard edges, not gradient), with a series of evenly spaced raised rectangular lug shapes around the rim, each lug flat-shaded with a small pale worn contact-polish patch (#9dbfa2 flat) on its face.

The membrane skin: dry dusty tan-brown (#7c6d5a flat body). It has a gentle catenary sag, but the sag is OFF-CENTRE and ASYMMETRIC — the deepest point of the sag sits about one-third of the way across the disc, not in the middle, and the shadow shape of the sag is an irregular lopsided pool, not a perfect concentric circle. Render the sag shadow as 2-3 flat posterized bands (#4f453a mid shadow, #2b2621 deepest, small area only) — not a smooth radial gradient. A faint flat pale dust texture (#8d7a5e small flat specks) scattered unevenly across the skin.

Forbidden: any three-quarter/angled/isometric perspective; any cast shadow, floor plane or backdrop; a centred or symmetric sag; a smooth gradient anywhere (all shading must be flat hard-edged bands); ink outline/contour lines beyond the flat color boundaries themselves; bright brass/gold color; glowing anything; text, icons, or UI glyphs.
